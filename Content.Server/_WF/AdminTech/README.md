# AdminTech

The Bluespace and Subspace Technician: a physical staff body for when an admin needs hands, ported from Meridian
Rift's `admin_tech` module (SS13). It is content on existing components plus the few behaviours the gear needs.
The staff marker, the observer and its kit are the `AdminObserver` module's.

How it is used: Spawn as Outfit lists the two technician outfits first, under Staff. Spawning yourself gives you
Return, an action that deletes the body and puts you back in your earlier body, observing from where the
technician stood, or in a new observer if you had no body. Spawning another player asks first whether they get
Return. Both outfits are also stock avatar presets, so an admin observer can manifest as either without a body.
The tools are in the observer's kit catalogue.

## The body

Each piece of worn gear gives one power while worn and takes it back when it comes off
(`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/AdminTech/clothing.yml`):

| Power | Piece | With |
|---|---|---|
| Interaction at any range, instant do-afters | gauntlets | `BypassInteractionChecks`, `IgnoreUIRange`, tags `BypassInteractionRangeChecks` and `InstantDoAfters` |
| All access | ID card | child of `UniversalIDCard` |
| Damage immunity | shield projector (neck) | `Godmode` |
| No air needed | mask | `BreathingImmunity`, `PressureImmunity` |
| Zero-gravity grip | boots | child of `ClothingShoesBootsMagAdv` |
| Identity masked, every language, combined HUDs | visor | `IdentityBlocker`, `UniversalLanguageSpeaker`, the security and medical HUD parents, `ShowAntagIcons`, `ShowSyndicateIcons` |
| Every radio channel | headset | child of `ClothingHeadsetAltCentCom` with more keys |
| Staff marker | uniform | `WFStaffUniform` |
| Cannot be stripped | every piece | `WFAdminGear` |

Components are granted by Goobstation's `ClothingGrantComponent`, which leaves alone a component the wearer
already had. Tags are granted by `WFGrantTags`, which does the same for tags and takes several. `WFStaffUniform`
marks the wearer with the `AdminObserver` staff marker: wording, colour and sigil come from the wearer's player,
and are resolved again when a player takes the body over.

`WFAdminGear`: only the wearer or an admin can take the piece off, and it says what it is on examine.

## Wire legend

A viewer of a wires panel who holds the subspace multitool (`WFWireLegend`) gets a second window listing what each
wire does, by colour and letter. The client asks when the panel opens and `WFWireLegendSystem` answers that viewer
alone; the panel's shared state is not changed, so nobody else sees anything.

## Art

`Tools/_WF/AdminTech/art.py` converts the module's own sprites from a Meridian Rift checkout into
`Resources/Textures/_WF/AdminTech`, with the source and credits in each `meta.json`. The uniforms, jackets, visors,
masks, gauntlets and shield projector use the art the `MeridianClothing` port already converted; behaviour goes on
child prototypes here, never in that module's generated files.

## Not ported

- The pluoxium tank and the three gas mixes: Wolfgate has no pluoxium, healium, nitrium, halon, BZ or helium.
- The handheld chem dispenser: a reagent dispenser here is a powered machine that draws from jugs in its own
  storage, which an item in the hand does not have.
- The job locker and vendor beacons: every choice in their menu needs a name and a description that nobody has
  written yet. Left for a follow-up.
- MODsuits and their modules, cyborg walkers and their items, the techweb fabricator and its designs, the
  omniplumber: each needs systems Wolfgate does not have.
- The medigun and cell-loaded guns, firing pins, the tiny-fan capsule, the plushie spawner boxes; the human
  spawner, which Spawn as Outfit replaces; the rapid lighting device, because the RCD already builds lights.
- The carbine keeps its fire modes from the multiphase energy gun it is built on, not the original thirteen.

Entry points: `WFAdminGearSystem`, `WFGrantTagsSystem`, `WFStaffUniformSystem`, `WFWireLegendSystem`
(`TryGetLegend`), `WFReturnSystem` (`Grant`, `Return`).
