# JudgeLine

A 911 line for vagrants. A vagrant (the overflow job, in no company) in the Colossus sector pays `wf.judgeline.fee`
(50,000) to call a Caelestinus judge ship to an emergency, from the Judge Line PDA app (installed on vagrants' PDAs
at spawn) or from the Judge Line verbs on any shuttle console. A caller on a station, owing the Bench, barred for
hoaxes or on cooldown is refused.

`WFJudgeLineSystem` queues calls for up to `wf.judgeline.responders` responders. A dispatched responder
(`WFEncounterJudgeResponder`: Hornet, Lightning, Petra or Roswell with a pilot, gunner, paramedic and two guards)
drops out of FTL `arrival_distance` from the caller after `dispatch_delay`, pinned so the encounter system leaves it
alone. On arrival it checks which emergencies are real: none, then or when the call was made, is a hoax (fee kept, a
strike, `hoax_cooldown`; three strikes bar the caller for the round). A real emergency other than the one chosen is
handled instead.

- Under fire: escorts the caller's ship as an ally and fights whatever hit it in the last 90 s.
- Boarded: docks and posts its guards beside the hostile NPCs aboard.
- Medical: docks so its paramedic treats the hurt aboard (up to 8 min).
- Stranded: docks and leaves a `WFJudgeLineReliefCrate` (fuel, plating, tools, air); waits up to 5 min for thrust.
- Adrift: comes alongside, lets the caller aboard and flies them to the nearest station.
- Assault: fights whoever hurt the caller lately and hostile NPCs within 20 m, posting its guards there.

After 30 s with nothing wrong (90 s after a fight), or 15 min on scene, the guards come home and the responder
undocks and jumps out. The fee is refunded only for a call cancelled while queued, a responder that can't be placed
or one lost before it arrives. A caller whose ship fires on the judges voids the call.

Admins: `wf_judgeline list | call <kind> [player] | end <id>`; `call` places a free call with no checks.
