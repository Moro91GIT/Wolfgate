# RadarMenu

A left click on a helm, cockpit or mass scanner radar opens a console-styled menu for the spot, contact or waypoint
under the cursor. A click on a world still lists its signals, and radars whose clicks aim guns (gunnery, drones, the
cockpit's aim) keep their clicks.

- **A spot:** autopilot to here, save a waypoint here.
- **A contact:** autopilot alongside (it stops 30 m clear of the hull, on our side), dock at…, hail, save a waypoint
  on it. The heading shows the contact's name as the radar labels it, with range and bearing.
- **A waypoint:** autopilot to it, delete it.
- **Always:** copy coordinates, recentre a panned radar, and at a helm, cancel the autopilot.

Flying, docking, hailing and cancelling need a helm; waypoints need a helm or mass scanner on a grid. Keys a radar can't
use are greyed out.

**Dock at…** asks the server which free docks on the contact the ship can dock with (`WFAutoDockOptionsRequestMessage`)
and lists them nearest first, with **Any free dock** on top. The autopilot then flies to the ship's docked place, turned
as it will sit there. The helm's autopilot stops within 40 m, so on arrival the docking clamps pull the ship the rest of
the way in, as an FTL dock does. If the dock was taken meanwhile it takes any other that fits. If none does, the target
is gone, or the ship stopped more than 50 m short, the autopilot stays off and the pilot is told why. A ship that is
already docked must undock first.

Entry points: `WFAutoDockSystem` (server), `ShuttleNavControl.RadarMenu.cs` and `WFRadarContextMenu` (client); the
click reaches the menu through the radar's existing click handling in `ShuttleNavControl.PlanetSignals.cs`.
`WFAutoDockTest` covers listing, docking on arrival and the failures.
