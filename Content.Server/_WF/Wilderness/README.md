# Wilderness

Shows players where the rules protect them, and marks those who stay where they don't. A `wfZone` prototype is a kind
of zone (Safe Zone, Wilderness, faction outpost, not a safe zone) with its banner text, colour, icon, alert and the
rule it links to. A `WFZoneSource` on a grid, or a `WFZoneMarker` point on a planet, centres one: the Safe Zone on
Caelestinus Central (256 m), a Wilderness on every bluespace event site, Monolithic Fragment and planetary vault
(512 m), outposts on Halcyon and Helios (256 m), not-safe on Camelot, Underground and the Jupiter (aboard only).

`WFZoneSystem` works out each player's zone once a second (the ship they're aboard, edge to edge, or them on foot),
sets `WFInZoneComponent` for their client, shows the alert and says it in chat. A ship, or a person not aboard one,
that stays `wf.wilderness.dwell_seconds` (15) in a Wilderness gets `WFSkullComponent`: held while there, running out
`wf.wilderness.skull_minutes` (10) after leaving. A marked ship has no protection anywhere, Safe Zone included.
`wf.wilderness.open_to_all` says whether anyone or only TSF and PDV may fight there; `wf.wilderness.skull_on_foot`
whether people on foot are marked. The guidebook's `<WFWildernessRule/>` writes both into the rules.

The client (`WFZoneHudSystem`) shows a banner at the top of the screen that opens the rule when clicked, a large title
on crossing in or out, a pulsing border in a Wilderness or while marked, and a skull over marked people. Radars draw
each zone's edge and a skull by every marked ship (`ShuttleNavControl.Wilderness.cs`). Admins can check and set the
mark with `wf_zone info|mark|clear`.

The zone icons are built by `Tools/_WF/Wilderness/make_icons.py` from the radar marker glyphs.
